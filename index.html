About Entergrate

A more inclusive Finland is built together.

Entergrate facilitates the professional and social integration of immigrants into Finnish society.

Our community includes working professionals, volunteers, and allies who connect immigrants with relevant organizations, employers, and local networks. We create the conditions for people to contribute their skills, build meaningful relationships, and enrich their lives in Finland.

Our work through the years
