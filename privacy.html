Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Entergrate does not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. We only store your cookie-notice choice in your browser.

Who we are

Entergrate is a volunteer-led association based in Helsinki, Finland. We support integration, inclusion, community dialogue and professional participation.

Browser storage

When you respond to the cookie notice, your choice is saved in your browser using local storage under the following name:

entergrate-cookie-choice

This prevents the notice from appearing every time you visit. The stored value does not identify you and is not sent to Entergrate. You can remove it by clearing this website’s data in your browser.

Contact form

If you use our contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number and message. We use this information only to receive and respond to your enquiry.

The contact form is processed through Formspree. Formspree may process the submitted information on our behalf to deliver your message.

External websites

Our website contains links to services such as Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies after you follow those links.

How long information is kept

Contact information is kept only for as long as necessary to respond to the enquiry or meet applicable administrative and legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to applicable data-protection law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also object to or request restriction of certain processing.
